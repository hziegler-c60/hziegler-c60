<h2>Hi, I'm Hannah.</h2>
</header>

<p>I care about preserving information and making it accessible to the people who need it. My path has taken me from library technical services, where I helped rebuild an entire public library collection after a fire, to co-founding a Chicago catering business with donation-funded meal events for local families. Across every role, the common thread has been building systems that connect people with resources.</p>

<p>I'm currently a Cycle 60 intern with I.C.Stars, where I'm growing my skills in data analysis, HTML, CSS, and SQL, and working on projects like my Kinect-based 3D modeling project that you can learn more about on <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/projects/index.html">The Science</a> page.</p>

<p>With a Bachelor's in English and Philosophy, I also bring a love of reading, research, and critical analysis to everything I do — and I'm passionate about engaging with media in all of its formats. Some of my own writing can be found on <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/art/index.html">The Arts</a> page.</p>
